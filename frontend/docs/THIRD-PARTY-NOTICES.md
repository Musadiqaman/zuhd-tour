# Third-party content

The Google reviews area loads the official Elfsight platform from `https://elfsightcdn.com/platform.js` only when a valid widget App ID is configured and the section approaches the viewport. The provider supplies review content and its attribution. The platform script is not copied, modified or self-hosted.

Google Maps and Elfsight are trademarks of their respective owners. Review content remains attributable to its authors. Configure the widget and use the service under your own Elfsight account and plan.

Tour imagery provenance is documented in IMAGE-NOTES.md. Runtime packages retain their upstream licenses in installed dependencies.
