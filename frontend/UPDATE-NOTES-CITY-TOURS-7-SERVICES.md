# City Tours page update

The `/dubai-city-tours` page now follows a simple category-directory presentation inspired by the information architecture of the referenced Gulf Dunes Doha City Tours page.

Displayed categories:
- Dubai City Tour
- Abu Dhabi City Tour
- Private Tours
- Dhow Cruise
- Tour Guide Service
- Transport Services (SUV cars, private cars, vans and buses)
- Parks Tickets

Desert Safari is intentionally NOT included on this page because it has its own dedicated page at `/desert-safari-dubai-tours`.
